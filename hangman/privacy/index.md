# Mono Hangman — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-04-12
**Last updated:** 2026-04-12

This Privacy Policy describes how **Mono Hangman** ("the App", "we", "our")
handles information about users of the App. The App is developed and published
by **sskplay** and distributed through Google Play.

## Overview

Mono Hangman is an offline single-player word puzzle. The App itself does not
require you to create an account, does not send game data to our servers, and
does not contain in-app purchases.

The App does, however, display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google
AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve ads. This Privacy Policy
covers both the App's own handling and AdMob's role as a third-party service.

## Information we collect ourselves

The App does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information to
our servers. The following data is stored **only on your device** and is never
sent to us:

- Your current level, score, and high score
- Hint credits
- Game progress state

You can erase this data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android
Settings or by uninstalling the App.

The App does not collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Your precise or approximate location (we do not use GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell
  network location)
- Your contacts, photos, files, camera input, or microphone input
- Crash reports, analytics, or usage telemetry
- Any unique identifier beyond what Google AdMob uses internally for ad delivery

## Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses **Google AdMob** (provided by Google LLC) to display
advertisements. To deliver ads, AdMob may access and process:

- The Android **Advertising ID**, a user-resettable identifier used for ad
  personalization and frequency capping
- Coarse device information (device model, operating system version, language,
  country)
- Approximate, non-precise location derived from your IP address
- Basic interaction data with the ads themselves

Google AdMob's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [AdMob help — ads personalization](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in
**Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**. From Android 12 onwards you can also opt
out of ad personalization from the same screen, which replaces your Advertising
ID with a string of zeros.

## Permissions the App requests

The App declares the following Android permission:

- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` — required by Google Play services
  to access the Advertising ID for AdMob.

The App does **not** request internet, location, storage, camera, microphone,
contacts, or any other runtime permissions of its own.

## Children's privacy

Mono Hangman is rated suitable for everyone. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Because the App displays AdMob ads, ad
content delivered by Google may be subject to Google's own policies on
advertising to children. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns,
please contact us using the details below.

## Data retention and deletion

- **On-device game state** is retained until you clear the App's data or
  uninstall the App.
- **AdMob data** is retained by Google according to its own retention policies;
  see the links in the Advertising section above.
- We do not operate any server-side database of user records because we do not
  collect any.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is no personal record
on our side for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. For data handled
by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights directly through Google using the
policies linked above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update
the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App
after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
