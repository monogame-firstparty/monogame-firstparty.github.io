# Mono & Watch

Ten tiny retro games in one pocket console, all built on the Mono fantasy
console: Snake, Pong, Sokoban, Hangman, Missile Defense, Fireworks, Slide,
Scorch, Potato Volleyball and Mono Girls. Pick a game on the CRT carousel and
play with the on-screen gamepad. Many of the games also have a WATCH mode where
the AI plays for you.

- [Privacy Policy](./privacy/)

## Premium

- **2h Premium** — watch one short rewarded ad for two hours of Premium.
- **Premium forever** — a one-time in-app purchase.

Premium removes all ads and unlocks landscape play and every screen style.
If you bought Premium forever and it doesn't show up, open Settings in the app
and tap **Restore purchases**.
