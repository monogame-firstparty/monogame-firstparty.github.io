# Privacy Policy — Mono & Watch

**Effective date:** 2026-09-26
**Last updated:** 2026-09-26

This Privacy Policy applies to **Mono & Watch** ("the App"), published by
**sskplay** ("we", "our") on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It replaces
our [general games policy](/mono/privacy/) for this App, because the App offers
an in-app purchase and runs on iOS as well as Android.

## Overview

Mono & Watch is an offline, single-player collection of games. It does not
require an account and does not send your game data to our servers. We do not
run any server that receives data from the App.

The App shows advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** and offers an
optional one-time in-app purchase ("Premium forever") processed by Apple or
Google.

## Information stored on your device

The following stays **only on your device** and is never sent to us:

- Game progress, scores, settings and the selected screen style
- Whether Premium is active (the 2-hour reward timer, and a cached record that
  you own Premium forever)

You can erase it at any time by deleting the App (iOS) or clearing its data or
uninstalling it (Android).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address,
contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera or microphone input, and we
do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

## Advertising — Google AdMob

When Premium is not active, the App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to show a
banner ad, an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a game, and optional
rewarded ads. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

- Your device's advertising identifier — the **IDFA** on iOS (only if you allow
  tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt) or the **Advertising ID**
  on Android
- Device information (model, OS version, language, country) and approximate
  location derived from your IP address
- Interaction data with the ads themselves, and ad performance and diagnostic
  data

**Your choices**

- **iOS:** the App asks for permission to track using Apple's App Tracking
  Transparency prompt. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and you still get
  (non-personalized) ads. You can change this at any time in
  **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad
  personalization, in **Settings > Privacy > Ads**.
- **EEA, UK and other regions that require consent:** the App shows Google's
  consent form before requesting ads. You can review your choice later from
  **Settings > Privacy options** in the App.
- **Premium** (2-hour or forever) turns all ads off.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## In-app purchase

"Premium forever" is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment
is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment details.
The App asks the store on your device whether you own Premium so it can unlock
it and restore it on a new device. We do not receive or store your purchase
history on any server.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Ads shown in the App are limited to
general-audience content.

## Data retention and deletion

- **On-device data** is kept until you delete the App or clear its data.
- **AdMob data** is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.
- We do not operate any server-side database of user records, so there is no
  personal record on our side to provide, correct or delete. For data handled
  by Google, use the Google policies linked above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update
the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
